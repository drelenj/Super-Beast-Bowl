# ARCADE-2 canon questions for the owner

Nothing here is decided. Each item names the **PROVISIONAL** default the arcade uses so the game keeps working, and where it lives in code. Only the owner rules on canon.

| # | Question | Why it came up | PROVISIONAL default in the arcade | Code |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Keeper reach.** Standing reach of large athletes (2.3 to 4.3 m in the workbook) exceeds the 3 m goal. Is a Keeper's reach limited? | Listed as open in the brief. | No cap: reach comes straight from the workbook (standing reach + jump; arm span for width). | `physics.ts: hReach, vReach` |
| 2 | **Kamasi Sinella.** The brief says he is an elite outlier with every rating 85+. The workbook has Speed 71, Strength 77, Jumping 74, Acceleration 72, Agility 85, Balance 81, Throw Power 80, Stamina 71, Durability 92 (OVR 90). Which is right? | Data and brief disagree. | Workbook values used unchanged (ratings are locked). | `data/rosters.json` |
| 3 | **Restart after a half goal or awarded goal.** Does the fouling team restart at its own 40 m line like any score, or does the offence keep the ball? | The Rulebook says restarts follow "every score" and lists the half goal as a way to score. | Treated as a score: conceding team scrimmages at its own 40 m line. | `engine.ts: defenceMinor` |
| 4 | **Goal-line toss timeout.** The 16 Nov ruling spots the restart 5 m out. Who restarts, as what, and is there a count penalty? | Ruling gives only the spot. | Same team, by scrimmage 5 m out, tackle count +1 (as a missed snap); turnover at count 1. Sideline toss timeouts are treated the same way, 5 m infield. | `engine.ts: missedSnap` |
| 5 | **Sudden-death period length.** The Rulebook gives a 1 minute break "before each sudden-death period" but no length. | Needed to schedule breaks and Hunts. | 300 s (same as the first overtime), repeated until any score. | `engine.ts: endPeriod` |
| 6 | **Replacement after a DQ.** "Once the exclusion window for that team has ended": how long is the window for a DQ? | Two DQ routes (second major, fifth minor). | 120 s for a second major, 60 s for a fifth minor; then a same-role reserve enters at the next dead ball. | `engine.ts: foul, benchWindow` |
| 7 | **Illegal arrow outside the Archer's own half.** The table penalises "shooting a frozen or immune player" (arrow forfeited, minor). Is an arrow at a player outside the own half the same? | Only one of the two illegal cases is in the table. | Same: arrow forfeited, minor on the Archer (a defence minor if his team is defending). | `engine.ts: fireArrow` |
| 8 | **Keeper Zone breach spot.** "Turnover at the 20 m line" against the general turnover rule (spot or own 40 m line, whichever is closer to the goal attacked). | The two rules give different spots. | 20 m line, as the specific rule says. | `engine.ts: rules` |
| 9 | **Major foul inside a Keeper Zone: "scrimmage at the goal line".** Where exactly? | No distance or lateral spot given. | 1 m in from that goal line, at the foul's lateral position. | `engine.ts: majorFoul` |
| 10 | **Timeout called in live play by the team in possession.** How does play restart? | Rulebook allows the call but gives no restart. | Scrimmage at the spot for that team; tackle count and play clock unchanged. | `engine.ts: takeTimeout` |
| 11 | **Hunt start positions.** Where do the two contestants stand? | Only "the ball rests still at the centre" is given. | Each contestant on his own 40 m line (10 m from the ball); everyone else behind it. | `engine.ts: setupHunt` |
| 12 | **Who is "immune" for the contact major.** Does "contact with a frozen or immune player" cover the 10 s freeze immunity (amber light), or only Keeper immunity? | Wording covers both readings. | Frozen and recovering players and an immune Keeper are protected; a player with only freeze immunity can be tackled. | `engine.ts: tryTackle` |
| 13 | **Ball specification.** Size, mass and drag are "engine standard", not canon text. | Needed for flight and the whole-ball goal test. | Radius 0.11 m, mass 0.45 kg, light drag, bounce 0.45. | `physics.ts: PHYS` |
| 14 | **Speeds below the top of the scale.** Rating 100 = 44.704 m/s is canon; the linear mapping below is a placeholder. Throw speed, acceleration, turn rate, jump, stamina and injury mappings are all arcade placeholders. | Brief asks for them to be isolated. | One constant or function each. | `physics.ts` |
| 15 | **Head contact and held ball.** Not modelled (no head-height contact and no mutual holding exist in the arcade). | Rules exist in the Rulebook. | Not implemented; listed in the rule matrix. | `docs/RULE_MATRIX.md` |
